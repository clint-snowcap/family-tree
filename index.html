⚓ How Cousin Trees Work

Reading the Side-by-Side Family Ladder

How to Read Each Step
  • ⚓ Shared Common Ancestor (Top Box): The exact grandparent couple that BOTH your family and the famous person descend from.
  • 👥 Step 1 (Children / Siblings): The two people directly below the Shared Ancestor are Brothers & Sisters (children of the Shared Ancestor). Your family descends from the sibling on the left; the famous cousin descends from the sibling on the right!
  • 🤝 Step 2 (Grandchildren / 1st Cousins): The children of those siblings—making them 1st Cousins to each other!
  • 🤝 Step 3 (2nd Cousins), Step 4 (3rd Cousins)...: Each step down adds one cousin degree.
  • ⏳ "Removed" Steps at the Bottom: When one branch reaches the Famous Person earlier in history (like Abraham Lincoln at Step 8) while your branch continues down to the present day, those extra generations at the bottom are the "Times Removed"!
Gateway Marriage • Dec 31, 1761

Lt. Henry Ingalls & Sybil Carpenter

On New Year's Eve 1761 in Rehoboth, Massachusetts, Revolutionary War Patriot Lt. Henry Ingalls (1738–1813) married Sybil Carpenter (1740–1833).

Proven by presidential genealogist Gary Boyd Roberts and the Rehoboth vital records, Sybil was the 2x great-granddaughter of Mayflower teenager Francis Billington and 3x great-granddaughter of John & Elinor Billington—while Henry descended from 1629 Lynn founder Edmund Ingalls.
